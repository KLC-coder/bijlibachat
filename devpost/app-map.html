BijliBachat App Map

One-page energy planning flow for a household user.

1. Add appliances

Use quick-add chips or the custom form to enter name, quantity, watts, and daily hours.

↓

2. Calculate load

Each item calculates daily kWh and 30-day usage; totals update instantly.

↓

3. Set target

Enter a desired monthly reduction such as 50 units and click “Reduce My Bill”.

↓

4. Protect essentials

Fans, study lights, and refrigerators stay protected while adjustable loads are evaluated.

↓

5. Get action plan

See a realistic daily savings schedule with exact runtime cuts and clear recommendations.
